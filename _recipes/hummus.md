tags:

directions:
- Cook 1 lb garbanzos with 8 cups of water, 1-4 cloves garlic, and 40 g salt for 75 min on high pressure. Release doesn't really matter.
- Blend garbanzos hot.
- Combine 4-6 cloves garlic with the juice of 2 lemons and blend.
- Add 1 cup tahini and blend. Add water as needed to get it moving.
- Combine lemon, garlic, and tahini with garbanzos.

components:
- 1 lb dry garbanzos
- 8 cups water
- 40 g salt

- 4-8 cloves garlic
- 2 lemons
- 1 cup tahini
